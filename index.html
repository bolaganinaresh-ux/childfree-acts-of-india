🌍 International child-rights foundation

UN Convention on the Rights of the Child
A global legal framework for children's rights. Childhood is treated as a special protected period and the Convention addresses protection, development, participation and dignity.

📚 Country-specific legal starting point

📋 What should I do now?

  1. Confirm the country and region.
  2. Describe the child's age and relevant circumstances without unnecessary personal data.
  3. Collect only documents actually required by the responsible authority.
  4. Verify the current law and eligibility using an official source.
  5. Contact the responsible child-protection, education, health, legal-aid or emergency authority.
Important: This is an educational prototype, not legal or medical advice. It does not diagnose conditions, decide legal eligibility or replace a lawyer or doctor. Laws and procedures change.
